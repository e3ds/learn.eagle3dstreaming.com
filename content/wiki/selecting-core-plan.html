Follow the steps below to purchase the Core Plan:

Step 1. Sign In to the Control Panel

Open the Control Panel and sign in using your account credentials.

Step 2. Navigate to Plans and Select the Core Plan

From the Control Panel, navigate to Plans and select the Core Plan.

You can choose between:

  • Monthly Plan

  • Yearly Plan

Select the billing option that best suits your requirements.

Step 3. Click Continue

After selecting the Core Plan and your preferred billing cycle, click Continue to proceed with the subscription.

Step 4. Enter Your Payment Information and Click Subscribe

Enter the required payment information:

  • Credit card details

  • Cardholder name

  • Country

  • ZIP Code

After entering the required information, click Subscribe to complete your purchase.

Once the payment is successfully processed, you will be automatically redirected to the Payment Successful page.

Step 5. Go to the Streaming Apps Page

After successfully subscribing to the Core Plan, navigate to the Streaming Apps page from the Control Panel.

From there, you can upload and manage your applications and start using the streaming service.

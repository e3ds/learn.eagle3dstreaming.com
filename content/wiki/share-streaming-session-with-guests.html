This guide explains how to share your streaming session with team members, colleagues, classmates, or clients. Using the Guest URL, others can join your session in view-only mode without controlling the application.

Overview

The Guest URL allows multiple users to:

  • View your live streaming session in real time

  • Follow your interactions and workflow

  • Join instantly via browser without setup

Note: Guest users cannot control the stream — only the host retains control.

Step-by-Step Guide:

Step 1. Access the Streaming URL

Open your application using the main streaming URL.

Step 2. Click the Share Button

Locate and click the Share button in the streaming interface.

Step 3. Copy and Share the Guest URL

Copy the generated Guest URL and share it with your intended audience.

When a guest opens the link:

  • Your original streaming session acts as the host session

  • Guests can only view the session

  • Guests cannot interact or control the application

Real-World Use Cases

This feature is ideal for:

  • Education & Training
    Instructors can demonstrate workflows while students observe in real time

  • Team Collaboration
    Share live progress with team members without giving control access

  • Real Estate Demos
    Showcase property walkthroughs to multiple clients simultaneously

  • Product Demonstrations
    Present applications or prototypes to stakeholders

Step 4. Test the Guest URL

Click the Test button to verify the Guest URL.

This will open a new browser tab displaying the guest view.

Step 5. Session Behavior

If the host closes the main streaming session:

  • All connected guest sessions will automatically end

Important Notes

  • Guest access is view-only

  • Host controls the entire session

  • Guest sessions are dependent on the host session

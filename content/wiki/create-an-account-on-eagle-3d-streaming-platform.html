Follow the steps to set up an account:

Step 1. Access to the Sign Up page.

Click this link to access to the Sign Up page


Step 2. Enter your Email and press CONTINUE button.

You can also Sign Up with Google

Step 3. Create a Password and press CONTINUE button.

Step 4. Enter username and press CONTINUE button.

Step 5. Enter OTP and press CONTINUE button.

Collect the OTP from your Email Inbox

Check Spam if you do not see the Email in your Inbox!

Step 6. Congratulations! You have created an account in Eagle 3D Streaming. Now you can see the control panel.

Step 7. Go to New Control Panel.

Step 8. In the New Control Panel, you’ll find a demo app. Follow the images below to stream this demo app.

After clicking “Play App,” the loading screen will transition through a few phases. No need to worry about these phases—just wait for the “Play” button to appear.

Congratulations! You’ve successfully streamed the default demo app. In the next section, we’ll guide you through uploading and streaming a pre-packaged sample app.

Upload an application we have already packaged, and watch it stream. This proves your account, the platform, your network and your browser all work together — before any build of your own is involved.

It is worth doing even if you are impatient to stream your own project. Packaging an Unreal application takes time and has its own ways of going wrong; if you meet a problem after doing both at once, you will not know which half caused it. This takes a few minutes and removes that question.

Before you begin

  • An Eagle 3D Streaming account — create one if you have not.
  • The sample application: download the ZIP. Upload it exactly as it is — do not unzip it.

Upload it

  1. Sign in to the Control Panel

    Open newcontrolpanel.eagle3dstreaming.com and sign in.

  2. Press Add Application

  3. Choose the ZIP you downloaded

    Press Browse and pick the file, or drag it onto the window. Upload the ZIP itself; the platform unpacks it.

  4. Name it and start the upload

    Give the application a name and press upload. Two optional settings sit here: a thumbnail image, and Send Updates On Email, which mails you when the app is ready rather than making you watch the page.

  5. Wait for it to be prepared

    The Control Panel shows an estimated time. The platform is unpacking the build and getting it onto a machine, so this is not only the upload — it continues after the file has finished transferring.

You will know it worked when

The application appears in your Control Panel and is no longer marked as preparing.

Stream it

  1. Open the app and create a stream link

    Click the application, then Create New Stream Link. The link is what you would send to someone else — anyone who opens it gets the stream, with no account of their own.

  2. Press Play App

  3. Wait for a machine

    A GPU machine is being started for your session. The loading screen shows while that happens; the first start of the day is usually the slowest.

  4. Press Play

    Browsers will not start audio and video on their own, so the stream waits for one click from you. This is a browser rule, not a setting — it is why every stream begins this way.

You will know it worked when

The application is running in your browser and responds to your mouse and keyboard. Everything you see is video from a machine in our cloud.

Try the same link on your phone. Nothing changes on the platform side — the same link works, and the stream adapts to the smaller screen. It is the quickest way to see what your own users will get.

Removing it afterwards

The sample has done its job once you have seen it stream. Deleting it keeps your Control Panel to the applications you actually care about.

  1. Open the application

  2. Press Delete App

  3. Confirm

    Deleting is permanent. To stream it again you would upload the ZIP once more.

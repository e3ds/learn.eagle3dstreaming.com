Upload a prepared demo build and a ready-made patch, and watch the app change — the quickest way to understand patching before your own project is involved.

You need

The packaged demo project, which contains both the build and a patch file. No Unreal Engine required.

  1. Upload the ZIP to the Control Panel as an ordinary app — see uploading from the Control Panel.

  2. Play it and note what it looks like. This is the "before".

The patch upload URL

Patches do not go through the normal upload page. You construct their address from your username and your app name: 

https://upload-old.eagle3dstreaming.com/patch/{userName}/{appName}/latest

So for username demo and app patchDemo: 

https://upload-old.eagle3dstreaming.com/patch/demo/patchDemo/latest

Both parts must match exactly. The username is the one you sign in to the Control Panel with, and the app name is the one you gave when uploading — not the display title. A mismatch gives you a page that accepts a file and patches nothing.

  1. Open that URL.

  2. Choose the .pak file from the extracted demo ZIP.

  3. Upload it.

Play the app again

It should now show the patched content — without the base build having been re-uploaded. That difference, between a few megabytes and the whole package, is the entire point of the mechanism.

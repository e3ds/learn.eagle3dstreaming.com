Follow the steps below to create a new meeting link and join the meeting both as a host and a guest.

Step 1. Login to the Control Panel

Step 2. Select the app you want to stream

Step 3. Navigate to Meeting Links

Step 4. Click on Create New Meeting Link

Step 5. Play the App as Host

You are now hosting the meeting and can manage participants

Step 6. Copy the Guest Link and Share

Share this link with the users you want to join as guests.

Step 8. Manage Participants

You can view the participant list and remove any guest if needed.

Host Can Remove a Guest

  • To remove (kick out) a guest from the stream, click the Kick Out icon located next to the guest’s name (Figure 12).

Allow Guest to Control the Stream

  • To grant a guest control of the stream, click the Allow icon located to the right of the guest’s name (Figure 14). Once enabled, the icon will change from white to red, indicating that control has been granted.

  • To revoke a guest’s control access, click the Allow icon again. The icon will revert from red to white, indicating that control has been removed.

White Icon: Guest is not allowed to control the stream.

Red Icon: Guest is allowed to control the stream.

This guide explains how Eagle 3D Streaming selects the datacenter that serves users, how routing works when the nearest region is unavailable, and how region availability differs between subscription plans. Understanding this process helps you estimate latency and predict how users connect from around the world.

Region Availability by Plan

The following regions are available depending on your subscription plan:

Note: Trial and Core plans currently support deployments in USA and Europe. Enterprise plan can be deployed in any supported cloud region worldwide, allowing you to host your application closer to your users for lower latency and better performance

How Routing Works

When a user starts a streaming session, Eagle 3D Streaming automatically selects the most appropriate datacenter. Users do not need to manually choose a region.

The routing decision is based on:

  • The user's geographic location

  • Distance to available datacenters

  • Available streaming capacity

  • Streamer machine availability

  • Subsciption Plans

Users connecting from any country are automatically routed to the closest available region (USA or Europe).

Note: If the nearest region has no available streaming capacity, Eagle 3D Streaming automatically routes the user to the next closest available region to ensure the streaming session can still start.

Best Practices

  • Choose a deployment region closest to your primary audience.

  • Estimate where most users will connect from before selecting a plan.

  • Contact Eagle 3D Streaming Team if your audience is concentrated in a specific region, as Enterprise deployments can be customized.

You can also select a preferred deployment region from your application configuration. Region selection has some limitations depending on your subscription plan. Refer to the Select Region documentation for more details.

The stream monitor

Shows every active session on your account, live, and lets you remove a user from one.

That matters for three reasons, and only the first is obvious:

  • Sessions occupy slots. Your plan allows a certain number of concurrent users, and a session nobody is using still holds one. Ending it frees capacity immediately.
  • Sessions cost minutes. Streaming time is billed whether or not anyone is watching, so an abandoned session is spending money — see plans and billing.
  • Not everyone streaming should be. A shared link reaches whoever it is forwarded to, and this is how you remove someone who has it and should not.

Removing users by hand does not scale, and it is not meant to. Use it for the exception. For the ordinary case — people leaving tabs open — set an idle timeout and let it happen automatically.

Notifications

The Control Panel raises notifications about your account and applications — uploads finishing, and things needing attention. Worth checking after starting a large upload, since it tells you the app is ready without you watching the page.

Analytics

The Analytics section shows how much you have streamed over time. Use it to answer the questions that decide a plan: when your peak actually is, how much of your allowance is used, and whether usage is growing.

Look here before changing plan

Concurrent users and minutes are different limits and are hit for different reasons. Analytics tells you which one you are actually short of — upgrading for the other one costs money and changes nothing.

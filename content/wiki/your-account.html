Your profile, your password, your email address, and the people you share an account with.

Profile

Under Manage Account: your display details and a profile logo. Cosmetic, and worth setting if colleagues share the account — it is how they tell whose it is.

Password and email

  • Changing your password is done from your profile.
  • Changing your email address changes where account mail goes — including the crash reports you may have configured in developer options. Worth checking those still point somewhere someone reads.

Your username appears in your streaming URLs. It is part of the address of every app you publish, so it is visible to anyone you send a link to. If that matters, anonymising the URL hides it without changing your account.

Deleting your account

This takes everything with it

Your applications, their versions, and every stream link you have shared. Anyone holding one of those links loses access at that moment. There is no undo, and re-registering does not bring any of it back.

Teams

Several people can work in one account. Invitations go out by email; the recipient accepts or declines, and once they accept they reach the same Control Panel you do.

  • Invite a member from the team section.
  • Remove a member when they no longer need access.
  • An invited person may decline, and nothing changes if they do.

Team members share the account, not a copy of it. They see your applications, your configurations and your usage, and what they do counts against the same plan. Removing someone is the way to end that — changing your own password does not, because they have their own sign-in.

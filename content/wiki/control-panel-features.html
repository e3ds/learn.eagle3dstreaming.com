Streaming on a phone or tablet

Nothing special is required. Your app streams to a mobile browser the same way it streams to a desktop one — open the streaming URL and the session runs through the same phases: requesting a slot, waiting for a machine, launching, then the play button, then video.

The play button is not optional on mobile, and it never will be. Mobile browsers refuse to start audio or video before someone taps — that is the browser's rule, not ours. A stream that appears stuck on the play screen is waiting for a tap, not failing.

What to check if a phone streams badly

Almost always the network rather than the device: mobile connections vary far more than fixed ones, and the stream adapts by dropping quality. See network requirements for what the connection has to allow, and streaming interface for the controls a touch device gets.

Which to use

Almost always

Plugin 1

Works from Unreal 5.1 onwards, and it is the only one that supports communication between your app and the page. If you send data either way — and most integrations do — this is the only option.

Plugin 2

Only on Unreal 5.7 or newer, and only for plain playback. Choose it if your app is watched rather than talked to, and you specifically want Epic's newer architecture.

Enabling Plugin 2 on Unreal 5.5 or 5.6 will not stream at all. The combination is unsupported, and the failure appears during upload testing or when a viewer opens the stream — not at the moment you enable it, which is where the cause is obvious.

The question that decides it

Does your page send anything to your app, or receive anything from it? If yes, Plugin 1 — there is no version of Unreal where Plugin 2 supports that. If your app simply plays, and you are on 5.7 or newer, either works.

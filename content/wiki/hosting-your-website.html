In this section, we’ll guide you on how to host your website for free. You can use any hosting latform you prefer, but for this guide, we’ll demonstrate using Netlify, a popular free hosting service.

Follow the steps below:

Step 1. Create or Log in to Netlify

Step 2. Go to Builds and click “New site from Git”.

Step 3. Click Try Netlify Drop.

Step 4. Click browse to upload your project.

Alternatively, you can drag and drop the folder directly.

Step 5 Select the folder that contains index.html file.

Step 6. Click Upload.

Step 7. Click Open Production Deploy.

Step 8. Click Play button.

You can modify the website URL

Step 9. You can customize your site URL.

Go to Site Configuration → Change site name.

Step 10. Verify Customized URL

Your app will now be available from the customized URL.

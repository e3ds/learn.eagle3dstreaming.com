The Trial Plan lets new users explore the platform and test core streaming functionality before committing to a paid subscription. It is usage-based, not time-based, the trial never expires on a calendar date. It ends only once all 60 streaming minutes have been used, and no payment method is required to get started.

Trial Expiration & Unused Resources

The Trial Plan itself ends automatically only when all 60 streaming minutes have been used. Reaching the 5 GB storage limit does not end the trial, it simply blocks further uploads while streaming remains available until the minutes run out.

Limit Reached Effect

Effect

60 streaming minutes used

The trial ends. Streaming pauses automatically and cannot resume without upgrading to a paid plan.

5 GB storage used

The trial continues. No new applications or app versions can be uploaded, but existing streaming activity and remaining minutes are unaffected.

There is no automatic upgrade or charge when the trial ends, access is simply paused until the user chooses to upgrade.

No rollover: Unused trial minutes and unused trial storage are forfeited once the trial ends. Storage does not carry over to a paid plan, each paid plan begins with a fresh allocation.

Billing & Payment

  • The Trial Plan is completely free; no charges occur while on it.

  • A credit card is not required to start the trial, and no payment details are stored during it.

  • A valid payment method is required only at the moment of upgrading to a paid plan.

  • Billing is never automatic, users are charged only after explicitly selecting and activating a paid plan.

Upgrading to a Paid Plan

Users can upgrade at any time, during or after the trial. Upon upgrade:

  • The paid plan activates immediately.

  • Trial limits are replaced by the new plan's allocations.

  • Billing begins at the time of upgrade.

Plan Cancellation

The Trial Plan requires no cancellation step. It ends automatically once usage limits are reached, and since no payment details are stored, there is nothing to cancel. Users remain free to upgrade at any time if they wish to continue.

Summary

Follow the steps to change the password:

Step 1. Click on the CHANGE PASSWORD button

Step 2. Select on Confirm button

Step 3. Password reset link sent to email

Step 4. Click on the link to reset the password

If you do not receive the email in your inbox, please check the spam folder.

Step 5. Enter New Password

Step 6. Click on CHANGE PASSWORD button

Step 7. Password Reset Successful

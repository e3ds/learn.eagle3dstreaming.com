When embedding an Eagle 3D Streaming (E3DS) application into your website, you may want the experience to reflect your own brand rather than the default Eagle 3D Streaming visuals (logos, loading icons, and other interface elements).

This process is called Whitelisting or more formally, Customizing UI Elements using the E3DS Control Panel.

Whitelisting allows you to:

  • Replace the default Eagle logo with your own company logo.

  • Customize the loading background, icons, and styles to match your branding.

  • Deliver a seamless, white-labeled experience to your users.

How Whitelisting Works

Whitelisting is managed through the E3DS Control Panel. You can log in and configure branding options for your application without touching code.

Key customizable elements include:

  • Logo (shown in the top bar or loading screen)

  • Loading background and animation

  • Queue screen visuals

  • Play button style and text

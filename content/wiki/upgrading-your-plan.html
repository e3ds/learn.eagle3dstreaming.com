Eagle 3D Streaming offers paid plans for users who need more streaming capacity, storage, and concurrent sessions than the Trial Plan provides.

You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time during or after your Trial Plan. You do not need to wait until your trial minutes or storage are fully used.

Eagle 3D Streaming currently offers two paid plans:

  • Core Plan – Designed for standard-scale usage.

  • Enterprise Plan – Designed for large-scale usage.

Note: Core Plan includes 300 streaming minutes per month. You can purchase additional streaming minutes when needed. The exact Enterprise configuration may vary based on your requirements.

Follow the steps below to choose a plan:

Step 1. Sign In to the Control Panel

Open the Control Panel and sign in with your account credentials.

Step 2. Click ‘Upgrade Now’

If you have used all your available trial minutes or storage, click Upgrade Now to continue using the platform with a paid plan.

You can also upgrade to the Core plan even before the allotted minutes and storage of the trail plan are fully used. Refer to this document on how to select the Core plan.

Step 3. Select a Paid Plan

Open the Plan Selection page to review and select the plan that best fits your requirements.

Choose between:

  • Core Plan

  • Enterprise Plan

Choosing the Right Plan

Core Plan — Standard-Scale Usage

Choose the Core Plan if your application has standard-scale requirements, such as:

  • Development and testing

  • Small to moderate production deployments

  • Up to 10 concurrent streams

  • Storage requirements of up to 250 GB

  • A monthly allocation of 300 streaming minutes with the option to purchase additional minutes

Enterprise Plan — Large-Scale Usage

Choose the Enterprise Plan if your application requires large-scale capacity, such as:

  • A large number of concurrent users

  • Up to 1,000 concurrent streams

  • Up to 1 TB of storage

  • Higher-scale production deployments

  • Large commercial or enterprise applications

Set a Max session time from the Control Panel.

Eagle 3D Streaming does not impose a maximum streaming duration. You can stream continuously for 10 hours, 50 hours, 100 hours, or even longer, as long as you have sufficient streaming minutes remaining in your subscription and a stable internet connection.

This YouTube video is simply a demonstration of 10 hours of continuous streaming and is provided as an example only. It does not represent the maximum supported session length.

If a stream disconnects unexpectedly, it may be due to an application crash, a network issue, or your subscription running out of streaming minutes. Please contact Support for assistance in resolving the problem.

Prerequisites

  1. Access to the Control Panel.

  2. Eagle 3D’s User Account.

  3. At least one App Uploaded.

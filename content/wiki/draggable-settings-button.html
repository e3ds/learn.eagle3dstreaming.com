Why Enable the Draggable Settings Button?
Enabling the Draggable Settings Button allows you to reposition the settings icon anywhere on the screen during streaming, giving you full flexibility based on your layout and preferences.

Follow the steps to set up draggable Settings Button:

Step 1. Login to Control Panel

Step 2. Select a Streaming App

Step 3. Choose or Create a Config Name

Step 4. Edit the Selected Config

Step 5. Enable Advanced Options

Step 6. Navigate to the UI tab

Step 7. Enable Draggable Settings Button

Note: To make the settings button visible during streaming, you must also enable the Settings Control Bar option.

Step 8. Save the Changes

Step 9. Confirm Save Again

Step 10. Click Play App to start streaming with the updated settings

Step 11. Move the Settings Button During Streaming

Once your stream starts, click and hold the Settings Button, drag it to your preferred location, and release the mouse button to fix it in place.

Limitation

In this document, we'll guide you through setting up AutoPlay for your app using the Control Panel.

Important:

  • For AutoPlay to function, the user must interact with the loading screen at least once before streaming begins.

  • When AutoPlay is enabled, the sound might not work unless the user interacts with the loading screen (e.g., by clicking anywhere) before the video stream starts.

  • If you disable AutoPlay, sound will always work normally.

  • However, if you want both AutoPlay and sound, make sure the user interacts with the loading screen before streaming begins. This is a browser behavior and not specific to Eagle 3D Streaming.

Follow the steps below:

Step 1. Sign In to your Control Panel

Step 2. Select your App

Step 3. Generate a Streaming Link

See : Generate a Streaming Link for your App.

Step 4. Create a Config

See : Create a Config for each Streaming Link.

Step 5. Edit the Config

See : Edit your Config.

Step 6. Enable Advanced Options

Step 7. Click Streaming UI

Step 8. Enable AutoPlay

Reminder: The user must still interact with the loading screen at least once to trigger streaming.

Play your App automatically without a Play Button.

Step 9. Click Save

Step 10. Click Save (again)

Step 11. Run the game to check the Feature

1. Click Play App

2. The App starts automatically after Loading (No Play Button to click)

Now you are ready to Play your App automatically without a Play Button !

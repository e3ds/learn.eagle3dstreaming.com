Follow the steps below to play the game:

Step 1. Navigate to UE App and select the uploaded app

Step 2. Copy the streaming URL

Step 3. Open the streaming URL in two different devices or browser windows.

If you encounter any issues after accessing the streaming URL from the browser, refer to this documentation for assistance.

Step 4. Click on Play button when it appears

Step 5. Go to each device or window, press the backtick key (`) on your keyboard, and enter the command below and press enter from keyboard

open server IP:Port

For our server the command is:

open 172.7.191.81:28889

Step 6. Both players joined the server and can see each other.

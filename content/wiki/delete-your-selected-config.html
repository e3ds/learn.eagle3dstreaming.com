Learn how to delete a Config from the Streaming Link.

Prerequisites

  1. Access to the Control Panel.

  2. Eagle 3D’s User Account.

  3. At least one App Uploaded.

Instructions

Step 1. Sign In to the Control Panel

Login with your information.

Step 2. Select your App

Step 3. Click on 'Delete selected config'

Assuming you already have a Streaming Link with a Config selected.

Click on the Trash Can next to the Config to delete it.

Refer to this document to generate a Streaming Link and to this document to Create a new Config.

To Edit your Config, check this document.

1. Click Continue

2. A 'Default' Config is set

A warning sign ⚠️ is displayed (if you hover the mouse on it), to inform that the Link was modified.

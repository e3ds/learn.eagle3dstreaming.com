Delete your App (if needed) from the new Control Panel.

Instructions

Step 1. Sign In to your Control Panel

Login with your information.

Step 2. Select your App

Follow this tutorial to Upload Apps on the Control Panel.

Step 3. Click on the ‘Delete App’ Trash icon 🗑️

Warning ❌ 🚫

Don’t name your future uploads the same name of the deleted Apps. (That causes the App to never load and stream).

Step 4. Refresh the page

If the App still remains in the list after deletion, contact Support.

Now you are ready to delete your App !

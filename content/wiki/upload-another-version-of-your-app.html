Information about how to update your App.

Follow the steps below:

Step 1. Sign In to your Control Panel

Login with your information.

Step 2. Click on the update icon

Step 3. Select and Open your Archive

1. Click browse

2. Select the Archive and click Open

3. Click Save

4. Click Save again

5. The App starts uploading

6. The App is done uploading

To check the uploaded version :

Go to : Main Control Panel Interface > Select your App > click on the version drop down menu.

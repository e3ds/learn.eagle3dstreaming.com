Steps to view and manage account settings from the Control Panel.

Overview

Interface allowing users to view and update their username, modify their Email address or reset the password.

Navigation

Step 1. Use your log in credentials to sign in to the Control Panel.

Step 2. On the dashboard, click on profile icon

Step 3. In the My Account section, there are three tabs:

  • Account Settings

  • Subscriptions

  • Transactions

Step 4. Select the Account Settings page to see your profile

Username

You can’t change username

Email

Follow this guide to modify your Email address.

Password

Follow this guide to reset your password.

When you open the streaming URL, the process will take time due to the current infrastructure.


Why does it take time?

  • Linux based pixel Streaming is an expensive process due to low demand.

  • Building a dedicated infrastructure for Linux builds would be too costly compared to the current demand.

  • The infrastructure is not optimized for Linux builds at the moment.

What happens when you open the streaming link?

  1. A machine is automatically allocated.

  2. The machine starts.

  3. Your app is downloaded.

  4. The app starts streaming.

Result:

This process takes longer for Linux builds due to the lack of infrastructure optimization.

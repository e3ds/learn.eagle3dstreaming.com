Run your packaged app against Epic's signalling server on your own machine, before it ever reaches the Control Panel.

Why bother, when uploading also tests it

Uploading does test your app — at phase 6, after four other phases, and the answer comes back as a red bar rather than a log you can read. Testing locally puts the same failure in front of you in a terminal, in seconds, with no upload in between.

What it actually proves

That the build is valid, that Pixel Streaming is enabled in it, and that the app streams and accepts input. Almost every upload that fails at testing fails one of those three, and all three are visible locally.

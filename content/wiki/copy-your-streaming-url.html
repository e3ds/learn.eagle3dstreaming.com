Information about how to copy the Streaming URL from the new Control Panel.

Prerequisites

Make sure to :

  1. Have access to the New Control Panel.

  2. Have an Eagle 3D’s User Account.

Instructions

Step 1. Sign In to your Control Panel

Login with your information.

Step 2. Select your App

You need to have at least one Uploaded App.

Step 3. Click the ‘Copy Streaming URL’ icon 📁

1. Your App URL is copied to the Clipboard

Now you know how to Copy your Streaming URL !

See how to Stream your App.

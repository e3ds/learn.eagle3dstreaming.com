Information about the Delete Version Feature in the New Control Panel.

Prerequisites

Make sure to :

  1. Have access to the New Control Panel.

  2. Have an Eagle 3D’s User Account.

Instructions

Step 1. Sign In to your Control Panel

Login with your information.

Step 2. Select your App

You need to have at least one App Uploaded.

Step 3. Select the Version to be deleted

See this document to select the Version of your App and run it.

Warning ❌ 🚫

You cannot delete the latest version of your App.

Step 4. Click the Trash can to delete the Version 🗑️

By clicking this Feature, you are removing the selected Version permanently from the Control Panel.

Step 5. Refresh the page

Check the drop down menu to see if the Version got removed.

If the ‘delete Version’ Feature is not working, contact Support.

Now you know how to Delete a Version of your App !

A metaverse app is a long-running multiplayer app. There is no separate pipeline for it — the same upload, launch and streaming path applies.

If your Unreal project supports multiple concurrent users in a shared world state, it can be uploaded and streamed with no additional configuration. Follow the multiplayer path.

Every difference is in your application, not in the streaming

Persistence, avatar identity and continuous social state are things your backend maintains. Pixel Streaming's job — render on a server, deliver video, carry input back — is the same either way.

The one thing worth planning for is the always-on part. A persistent world means servers that run continuously rather than for the length of a match, which is a capacity and cost question rather than a technical one. Work it out before launch rather than after the first month's usage.

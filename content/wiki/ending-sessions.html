Two different endings, two different nodes: stop the stream, or close the viewer's browser tab. Both are triggered from inside your app.

NodeWhat happens to the viewerUse it for
Force End Session The stream stops. The page stays open, showing that the session ended. A natural finish — the end of a tour, a completed configuration, a signed-off review.
Force End Tab The browser tab itself closes. A kiosk or a full-screen experience where a dead page left on screen would look broken.
Either one frees the machine immediately

Which is the real reason to wire one up. A session that ends when your app knows it is finished stops consuming minutes and releases a concurrent slot straight away — rather than waiting out an idle timeout because someone walked away from the tab.

Ending the session

  1. Drop an E3DS_PS_actor into the level.

  2. In the Level Blueprint, add whatever should trigger the end — the demo uses the 6 key.

  3. Add a Construct PS Message To Force End Session node, and a Send Message To E3ds PS System node.

  4. Wire the trigger's Pressed pin into Send Message, and the Construct node's output into its Descriptor pin. Compile and save.

Closing the tab

Simpler — one node, no message to construct. Drag from your trigger's Pressed pin and add Force End Tab.

A browser may refuse to close the tab. Closing a tab the user opened themselves is restricted, and the rules differ between browsers. Force End Session is the reliable one; treat tab closing as a nicety that may not happen, and make sure your app has already ended the session so nothing is left running either way.

Checking it works

What you should see

In a remote editor session, click the viewport to give it focus and press your key. Force End Session leaves the page showing an ended session; Force End Tab closes the tab. If neither happens, the E3DS_PS_actor is almost certainly missing from the level.

Choosing the right cloud VM and GPU is essential for smooth pixel streaming. This guide covers the cloud providers, VM types, and GPU specs we support, and how to pick the right one for your app and event.

Two Ways to Set Up Your Streaming Infrastructure

Before anything else, decide which setup path works best for you:

Option 1. Bring Your Own VM

You already have (or want to set up) your own cloud VM on AWS, GCP, Azure, CoreWeave, or Oracle. In this case:

  • You provision the VM yourself under your own cloud account

  • You share your VM details (provider, GPU, VRAM, CPU, memory) with us.

  • We confirm whether your VM meets the hardware requirements for your app, and configure pixel streaming on top of it.

Option 2. Request Our VM

You’d rather not manage your own cloud infrastructure. In this case:

  • We provision and manage a VM on your behalf, on the cloud provider and VM type best suited to your app.

  • You only need to give us your App URL so we can determine the right specs.

  • We handle setup, hosting, and pixel streaming configuration for your event end to end.

Either way, the first step is the same: share your App URL with us so we can check the hardware specifications your app needs to stream properly.

Getting Started

Step 1. Share Your App URL

If you’re hosting an event with us, please provide the App URL first (regardless of which option you choose above). We use this to check the hardware specifications your app actually requires to stream. This lets us:

  • Confirm whether your requested/existing hardware (Option 1) is sufficient for the event.

  • Recommend or provision the right VM type and specs on your behalf (Option 2).

  • Flag alternative VM types or specs if your current selection would lead to poor performance.

Step 2. Tell Us Your Requirements

If you’re going with Option 1 (Bring Your Own VM), please provide:

Requirement

Description

Cloud Provider

Your preferred cloud provider (AWS, GCP, Azure, CoreWeave, or Oracle).

GPU

The specific GPU model your application requires.

VRAM

The minimum amount of GPU video memory (VRAM) required for your application to run smoothly.

CPU

The required CPU core count or performance tier.

Memory (RAM)

The amount of system memory required for your application and the Pixel Streaming pipeline.

If you’re going with Option 2 (Request Our VM), you don’t need to specify hardware yourself, just send us your App URL and preferred cloud provider (if you have one), and we’ll recommend and provision the matching VM type and specs from the options below.

Important

  • Don’t undersize your VM to save money: Cheapest ≠ Best Fit.

  • Underpowered hardware can cause lag, stutter, or stream failure during a live event.

  • Choose your VM based on your app’s actual requirements (verified via the App URL check), not price alone.

  • Not sure what you need? Ask us to confirm the minimum viable spec before picking a lower-cost option.

Choosing the Right Spec for Your App

  • Match GPU and VRAM to your app’s rendering/model requirements first, this is usually the biggest performance bottleneck for streaming.

  • CPU and memory should comfortably cover your app’s baseline needs plus overhead for the streaming/encoding pipeline, not just the bare minimum to launch the app.

  • If your app is borderline between two tiers, choose the higher tier for live events, a stable, well-performing stream matters more during an actual event than saving on hourly VM cost.

  • When in doubt, submit your App URL so we can verify before the event, rather than discovering a shortfall during the live stream. This applies whether you’re managing your own VM or asking us to provision one.

Network & Region Considerations

  • Bandwidth: Pixel streaming is bandwidth-intensive. Make sure the streaming VM has sufficient upload bandwidth, and that your viewers have a stable, reasonably fast internet connection.

  • Region: Choose (or ask us to choose) a VM region geographically close to your audience, this reduces streaming latency and improves the viewing experience.

  • Latency-sensitive apps: If your app requires real-time interaction (e.g., live control, low-latency input), region selection matters even more, request the closest available region to your primary audience.

Lead Time & Testing

  • Please share your App URL and setup preference (Option 1 or 2) at least a few business days before your event so we have time to verify specs, provision resources, and troubleshoot if needed.

  • We recommend a short test/rehearsal stream before the live event to confirm everything performs as expected.

Pricing

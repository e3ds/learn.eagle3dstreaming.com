Why Use Pre-packaged Sample App?

The pre-packaged sample app gives you to try out Eagle 3D Streaming without having to set up your own project. It lets you explore the platform, see how it works, and try pixel streaming in just a few simple steps.

Follow the steps to stream a pre-packaged sample app:

Step 1. Login to Control Panel

Step 2. Download this ZIP file

Step 3. Within the downloaded ZIP file you can rename the executable (.exe) file.

Step 4. Click Add Application.

Step 5. Click browse.

You can also use Drag and Drop method

Step 6. Select the downloaded ZIP file.

Step 7. Give a name of your App and click the upload button.

You can also give a thumbnail of your app. You can enable the Send Updates On Email button to get alert by an email when the app is ready to stream.

Step 8. Wait some times(Est. Time will be shown in the control panel).

Step 9. You can see your App in the control panel.

Step 10. Click on the app and click Create New Stream link.

Step 11. Click on Play App to stream your App.

Step 12. Waiting for machine.

Step 13. Press Play button.

Step 14. Now you can see the app is streaming.
